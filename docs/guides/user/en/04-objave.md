# Posts

## New post

1. Open the brand → **Posts** tab.
2. Pick a **Channel** and in **What should we post?** write the topic, the focus and any offer. Facts belong in the CGP or the materials.
3. Click **Create post**.

Postaja writes the post from the CGP and materials and checks it against the platform, channel and brand rules. If it breaks a rule, Postaja fixes it once by itself.

## Editing and rules

- You edit the text on the post page and click **Save changes**. A counter shows characters and hashtags.
- If a post breaks a rule (too long, banned word, missing CTA …), it is marked **needs review** and you see what is wrong.
- **Copy text** copies the post text.

## Repeated topics

Postaja compares every written post with the brand's posts from the last 180 days (ready, needs review, approved, published).
- If the topic is similar to an earlier post, the post shows a warning with a link to it and the similarity percentage.
- If it nearly repeats it, the post is set to **needs review**. If that is intended (e.g. a follow-up on the topic), **Approve** it anyway.

## Post statuses

- **planned**: a slot without text. Click **Write text with AI** or write it yourself.
- **ready** or **needs review**: the text is written.
- **approved**: ready to publish.
- **published**: you mark it with **Mark as published** once you publish it. In the field next to the button you can paste the **Link to the post**; you can add or correct the link later on the post page (**Save link**). An **open post** link also appears next to the status in the brand's post table, in **Posts** and in the plan (**History** tab); it opens in a new tab.
- **skipped**: will not be published.

You change the slot (day and time) in the **From the plan** section.

## Images

For images the brand needs a [visual identity](/app/help/vizualna-podoba).

1. On the post click **Make images**. Claude picks a template, writes the text and the illustration description, and Postaja draws the images.
   - **Words on the image** complement the post text rather than repeat it: a short headline that draws people in to read. If the plan specifies text for the image, that text is used.
   - If the post in the plan has an **Image prompt**, the first image is an illustration made exactly from that prompt, unchanged (the brand's past posts are not used as a model for it).
   - The illustration never contains words or a logo; Postaja draws the brand logo and the words over it from the template. Illustrations drawn after the brand's past posts are checked by Claude; if the model draws made-up letters or a logo into one, it is drawn again automatically, without using credits.
2. **Words on the images**: correct the words and click **Save and refresh**. The images are redrawn at no extra cost. Words in \*asterisks\* are highlighted.
3. **What should AI fix on the images?**: describe the fix in words and click **Fix the images**. You pay only for the illustrations that change. If you write that something in a picture should not be there (e.g. "there is made-up text on the picture"), that illustration is drawn again even when the prompt stays the same.
4. **New images** makes everything again.

Nothing gets lost: every new creation, revision or word refresh saves the previous images as a version. In the **Earlier image versions** section you restore a version (**Restore this version**; the current images become a version) or delete it permanently (**Delete version**).

For a LinkedIn carousel, **Download PDF carousel** is available.

**Partner logo**: if the brand has [partner logos](/app/help/brand#files), on the post you pick a partner (or **None**) and click **Save logo**. The partner's logo is drawn in place of the partner's name on the image or, if the image doesn't contain it, next to the brand logo. If images already exist, they are redrawn free of charge with the same illustrations; the previous ones stay as a version.

For a brand with a persona, next to the **Make images** button you choose whether the illustrations show the persona (**With the persona …**) or not. See [Persona](/app/help/persona#persona-in-post-images).

## Animation

You can animate any image of a post: Claude designs the motion (when the headline, text, lines and logo appear, how the illustration moves), and Postaja draws every frame.

1. In the **Animation** section pick an image.
2. Optionally write **Animation instructions** (e.g. “headline word by word, 5 seconds”).
3. Click **Animate**. The button shows the maximum price.

The result is an MP4 video (3–10 s). Every video you create **stays on the post**: a new animation is added to the earlier ones (including persona videos), and new images do not delete videos. Each video has **Download (MP4)** and **Delete**; deleting is permanent.

## Client approval

An agency can send posts to its client for review without the client needing a Postaja account.

1. On the brand, **Posts** tab, in the **Client link** section, enter who the link is for (e.g. "Polygon – Ana") and choose the days (next week by default, at most 31 days). Click **Create link**.
2. Copy the link (**Copy**) and send it to the client. Postaja shows it only once; if you lose it, create a new one.
3. Through the link the client sees the brand's posts for those days (texts, images, videos). For each one they click **Approve**, or write a comment and click **Changes needed**.
4. An approval marks the post **approved**; a change request moves an already approved post back to **ready**. You see the client's feedback on the post page (**Client feedback**) and in the posts table ("client: approved" or "client: changes").

The link works until 14 days after the last day. **Revoke** stops it at once. Next to the link you see when the client last opened it.

## Download

**Download ZIP** on the post contains the text (`besedilo.txt`), the first comment, images, all videos (`animacija-1.mp4`, `persona-video-1.mp4` …) and the PDF carousel, whatever the post has. Daily ZIPs in the plan and on the dashboard contain all posts of the day and `pregled.csv`.
