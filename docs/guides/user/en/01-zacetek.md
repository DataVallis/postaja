# Getting started

Postaja writes and prepares social media posts for several brands at once. From the brand's guidelines (CGP), materials and plan it creates texts, images, animations, ads and videos with an AI influencer. You then download the posts and publish them yourself.

## Signing in

1. Open Postaja and enter your email address.
2. Click **Send link**.
3. Open the sign-in link in the email. The link is valid for 10 minutes and works only once.

There is no password. You can sign in only if a Postaja administrator has added you to an organization.

## Organization and roles

All work happens in an **organization**. A Postaja administrator sets up the organization and its plan (limits and monthly spending cap); the owner invites members.

- **Owner** creates brands, edits the CGP, channels, files, visual identity and persona, and invites members.
- **Editor** creates and edits posts, images, animations, ads and videos. Can only view brand settings.

## Team and plan

In the menu **Organization → Team and plan** you see members, invitations and plan usage: brands, members, AI generations and AI spend this month, each with the plan's limit.

The owner:
1. In **Invite a member**, enters an email address, picks a role and clicks **Invite**. The invitation arrives by email and is valid for 7 days; on first sign-in with that address the person becomes a member.
2. Changes a member's role by picking a role and clicking **Change**.
3. Removes a member with **Remove**. A removed member loses access immediately.
4. Withdraws an invitation that has not been accepted yet with **Cancel invitation**.

An organization always has at least one owner. If the plan does not allow more members or brands, Postaja tells you; for a bigger plan, contact the administrator.

## Basic workflow

1. **Create brand** and add its CGP (instructions for the AI) and channels (Instagram, LinkedIn …). See [Brand](/app/help/brand).
2. **Upload files**: logo, fonts, documents and images of past posts.
3. **Create visual identity**: Claude designs image templates for the brand. See [Visual identity](/app/help/vizualna-podoba).
4. **Prepare posts**: one at a time, from an imported plan, or in bulk for the whole plan. See [Posts](/app/help/objave) and [Plan](/app/help/plan).
5. **Review, approve and download** the posts (a ZIP with text, images and video), then publish them.

## Dashboard

The first page after signing in shows:
- posts by status (ready, needs review, approved, published);
- today's planned posts with the **Download today (ZIP)** button;
- **Today's goal per channel**: how many posts each channel needs today, how many are missing, and the streak of consecutive days. The **Create** button writes the missing ones, the **Suggest ideas** button suggests new topics;
- AI spend this month per brand.

## Nothing gets lost

What you create (images, videos, passport pictures, ad copy) stays until you delete it yourself. Each new creation or revision adds a new version, and the earlier ones remain available. Deleting is always a separate step with a confirmation.

## Language and theme

At the top right you switch between Slovenian and English and between the light and dark theme.
