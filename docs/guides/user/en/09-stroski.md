# Costs

Postaja pays for AI services (Claude for texts and design, fal.ai for images and videos) by usage. Every call is recorded against the brand and the post.

## Where you see costs

- **Dashboard**: AI spend this month and out of what cap, spend per brand.
- **Post**: the post's cost at the bottom of the page.
- **Buttons**: for more expensive actions (bulk creation, animation, passport picture, video with the persona) the button shows the **maximum price** before you start.

## Credits

If your plan has monthly credits, every paid AI action uses credits:

| Action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Post text or ad copy | 1 |
| AI illustration (image, slide) | 2 |
| Image animation | 1 |
| Persona image or passport picture | 5 |
| Persona video 5 s / 10 s | 25 / 40 |
| Competitor research (find or analyse) | 5 |

The automatic text fix and helper calls (image plan, design, ideas, plan import) use no credits. The administrator can change the price list; the current one always applies.

**Monthly credits** are used first (renewed every month), then **bought packs**, valid for 12 months. You see the balance in **Team and plan** → **Credits**. At 80 % Postaja shows a notice at the top of the page; when credits run out, no new texts, images or videos are made until the next month or a top-up.

**Top-up:** the owner clicks a pack in **Team and plan** (500 credits for €25 or 2,000 for €80). The request comes to us; once the payment is confirmed, the credits appear on the same page.

## Monthly cap

Besides credits, the organization has a monthly safety spending cap in € and may also have a limit on the number of **AI generations** per month (a generation is one paid AI call: a text, an image or a video). The administrator sets both; you see usage in **Team and plan**.

Before every call Postaja reserves the highest possible cost; if that would exceed the cap, the call does not run and you see a message about the cap. Failed calls are not charged.

## Approximate prices

- Post text: a few cents.
- AI illustration for an image: €0.04–0.06.
- Image animation: one Claude call, a few cents.
- Persona passport picture and illustration with the persona: €0.15.
- Video with the persona: 5 s up to about €0.62, 10 s up to about €1.05.

Prices are approximate; you see the exact price on the button before you start.
