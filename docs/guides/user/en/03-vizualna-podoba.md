# Visual identity

For each brand Claude designs its own set of **image templates** (cover, quote, list, number, CTA …) from the CGP, your description, colours, logo and images of past posts. Postaja then draws every image itself, so Slovenian characters and the brand's fonts are always correct.

## Create the identity

1. Open the brand → **Visual identity** tab (owner).
2. In **How should the graphics look?** describe the style in your own words: mood, composition, what you like and what you don't want.
3. Click **Create visual identity**. It takes up to a minute.

You get the best result if you first upload a logo, a font and a few images of past posts in **Files**.

## Review and revisions

- You see the templates in a preview in 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.
- In **What should I change?** write a revision (e.g. “bigger headline, more white space”) and click **Revise**. Claude changes only what you write.
- **Create from scratch** makes a completely new identity from the description.

Every revision is a new **version**. With **Use** you choose which one applies to new images.
