# Persona (AI influencer)

A persona is the brand's recurring AI person. From its **DNA** and **passport pictures** Postaja generates the same person every time – for images and videos.

## Create a persona

Open the brand → **Persona** tab (owner). There are two ways:

- **Describe the person – AI fills in the DNA**: roughly describe the person you want, or paste or import an existing DNA (document). Claude fills in all 13 fields; everything you wrote is kept.
- **Fill in the DNA yourself**: you enter the fields by hand.

## DNA

The DNA has 13 fields, in English, because the image model reads it:

Gender · Age · Ethnicity / Skin Tone · Hair Style · Hair Colour · Clothing Style · Mood / Emotion · Environment / Setting · Camera Angle · Pose / Action · Lighting · Style / Medium · Extra Notes

Gender, Age, Ethnicity / Skin Tone and Hair Colour are required. In **Extra Notes** write the features that must always be visible (eyes, marks, jewellery). You can correct and save the DNA at any time; the change applies to new images and videos.

## Passport pictures

- **Create passport picture**: a photorealistic close-up portrait, made with a prompt that includes the whole DNA. The button shows the price.
- **New passport picture** makes another passport picture; it is added to the earlier ones and becomes the primary one. The earlier ones stay until you delete them (bin icon next to the picture).
- **Upload pictures**: add your own pictures of the same person (PNG, JPG, WebP, up to 15 MB) and pick the angle.
- At most 10 pictures in total. With the star you pick the **primary** picture, which all videos start from.

If the provider rejects a request, the reason is shown under the error. Change the DNA and try again.

## Persona in post images

Once the persona has at least one passport picture, the illustrations of post images can show the persona. Claude describes the scene and the image is made from the passport pictures, so it is always the same person. Postaja still draws the words and logo according to the brand's visual identity.

- On each post, next to the **Make images** (or **New images**) button, you choose **With the persona …**. If you untick it, the illustrations follow the visual identity's style as before. The choice is remembered for that post.
- You set the default for new posts (including bulk creation) in the **Persona** tab, section **Persona in post images (default)**, with **Turn on** or **Turn off**.
- An illustration with the persona costs €0.15 (Nano Banana Pro); the cost preview for bulk creation takes the default into account.

## Video with the persona

The video is made on a **post**: open a post of a brand with a persona (the **Posts** tab, or the links in the **Video with the persona** section of the **Persona** tab). The post page has a **Video with the persona** section, below **Images**.

1. Pick the length (5 or 10 s). Each shows its maximum price.
2. Optionally write **Scene instructions** (where it is, what the persona does, how the camera moves). The video has no sound, so the persona does not speak.
3. Click **Create video with the persona**. The video takes a few minutes; the page refreshes by itself.

Claude writes the scene from the post, the first frame is made from the passport pictures (same person), and Kling 3.0 brings it to life as a vertical 9:16 video (1080×1920). Next to the video you see the scene and the motion. Every video stays on the post and a new one is added to the earlier ones; you delete a single video with **Delete**. All videos are also in the post's ZIP.

## AI label

A persona is an artificially generated realistic person, so it must be labelled when published (EU Artificial Intelligence Act, Article 50; also the rules of Meta, TikTok and YouTube). Postaja helps with this:

- A post that shows the persona (in an image or video) displays a warning explaining where to turn on the AI label on the chosen network.
- The export (ZIP) adds a note at the end of the text: “The person shown is AI-generated.”
- Images and videos with the persona carry an AI label in the file's metadata; Instagram and Facebook can recognise it by themselves.
