# Ads

One ad is one concept for the chosen networks (Meta, LinkedIn, Google Display), with copy and images for every placement.

## New ad

1. Open the brand → **Ads** tab.
2. Pick the **Objective** (awareness, traffic, leads, sales), enter the **Landing page**, the **Offer** and, optionally, **Instructions for AI**.
3. Pick the networks and placements (feed, story/reels …).
4. Click **Create ad**.

Claude writes **3 variants** of the copy for each network, in that network's fields and character limits (headline, primary text, description, CTA button …).

## Reviewing the copy

- You edit the fields and click **Save copy**. Postaja checks lengths, banned words and the CTA button.
- The warning “Longer than the visible part” means the end is hidden behind “See more”.
- **Write again** makes new copy for the same concept.
- Earlier copy is not lost: when you replace it (with **Write again** or by saving changes), it is kept under **Earlier copy**. You can restore a version (the current copy becomes a version) or delete it.
- **Download copy.csv** downloads all the copy.

## Creatives

1. Click **Create images**. Postaja draws an image for every placement and variant from the brand's visual identity; text and logo stay inside the safe zones.
2. You correct and save the words on the images at no extra cost.
3. Every new round of images saves the previous creatives under **Earlier creative versions**. You can restore a version (together with its words on the images) or delete it. Nothing disappears until you delete it yourself.
4. **Partner logo**: pick a partner and click **Save logo**; the creatives are redrawn free of charge with the partner's logo next to the brand logo.
5. **Download ZIP (images + copy.csv)** downloads everything for uploading to the ads manager.
