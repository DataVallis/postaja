# Postaja — brand identity

## Name
**Postaja** — the daily stop where your posts wait for you: pick them up, publish, move on.
Wordmark always lowercase: `postaja.` (orange full stop = "done for today").

## Mark
A bus-stop sign that is also a **P**: the pole is the stem, the orange stop disc is the bowl.
Three dots fading to the right = posts departing to the networks.
Files: `mark.svg` (app icon / favicon), `logo.svg` (light bg), `logo-dark.svg` (dark bg).
Note: the wordmark in the SVGs uses live text (Inter Display Bold); outline it before print or external use.

## Colors (design tokens)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | #12172B | text, dark surfaces, icon background |
| `signal` | #FF5A1F | primary action, status "ready", brand accent |
| `paper` | #F4EFE6 | light surfaces, text on dark |
| `white` | #FFFFFF | app background (light theme) |

Status colors (UI): ready = signal, published = #1F9D6B, needs review = #E0A100, failed = #D63B3B.

## Type
- Headlines: Inter Display Bold, tight tracking (−3 %).
- UI / body: Inter Regular / Medium.
- Both cover č š ž ć đ.

## Voice (Postaja's own posts and UI copy)
Short, calm, practical. "Danes: 4 objave pripravljene." not "Wow, your content is ready! 🚀".
Slovenian first, English second. No hype words (revolucionarno, game-changer).
