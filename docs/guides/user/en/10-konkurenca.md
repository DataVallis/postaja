# Competitors

Postaja helps you see what competitors do and learn from it, not copy it. It reads only public pages and what you upload yourself; it never signs in to social networks.

## Find competitors

1. Open the brand → **Competitors** tab.
2. Optionally write a **Wish for the search** (e.g. “only Slovenian”, “agencies too”, “no big platforms”).
3. Click **Find competitors**. Using the CGP and web search, Claude suggests up to 10 competitors: website, public profiles and why each is a competitor. The search takes a few minutes; the button shows the maximum cost.

Suggestions are under **Suggestions**. Check the website and the reason, then click **Keep** or **Remove**. A new search does not suggest those already on the list.

## Add your own competitor

Under **Add a competitor** enter the name, website and, optionally, one public profile (e.g. LinkedIn). The same competitor cannot be on the list twice.

## Screenshots

Instagram and LinkedIn don't show posts without signing in, so the most reliable source is what you see yourself. For each kept competitor click **Add screenshots** and upload screenshots of their posts, ads or carousels (PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 20 per competitor). You delete a screenshot with **Delete**.

## Analysis

1. Click **Analyse competitors**. Postaja reads the public websites of the kept competitors, and Claude compares them, together with the screenshots, against the CGP. The button shows the maximum cost. For each competitor you see whether the website was read.
2. The analysis shows a summary, **We adopt** (patterns that fit the CGP), **We avoid** (patterns that don't fit), **Topic gaps** and competitor profiles. Every point comes with evidence (website or screenshot).
3. Click **Yes** on the points you want and **No** on the others. Clicking again clears the choice.
4. The owner clicks **Send accepted to the CGP**. This creates a CGP proposal with a new section “Iz analize konkurence” (From the competitor analysis); the active CGP does not change until you insert the proposal into the editor in **Profile (CGP)** and save a new version.
5. On a gap, click **Suggest ideas**: the ideas form opens with the gap as the wish.

**Analyse again** makes a new analysis; earlier ones remain and you open them under **Earlier analyses**. Nothing is copied: Postaja adopts only patterns, never competitors' texts or images.
