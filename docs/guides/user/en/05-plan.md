# Plan

## Calendar

**Plan** in the menu shows the posts of all brands by day (month, week, day).
- You filter by brand, platform and status (**Show:**). Published posts are hidden by default.
- Clicking a day opens the day view; clicking a post opens it.
- **Download day (ZIP)** downloads all posts of the day.
- Tabs **No slot**, **History** (published) and **Creation** (bulk creation runs).

## Importing a plan

1. **Plan import** → drag in a plan (Excel, CSV, Word, PDF, MD or TXT, up to 20 MB). Several sheets and platforms in one file are fine.
2. AI recognises the columns or posts; values and texts are read verbatim. Correct the column meanings and click **Read again** if needed.
3. Pick the **Brand** (or create a new one) and a **Channel** for each platform. You can create missing channels from the profiles in the plan.
4. If the plan has no dates, pick a start day and the spacing.
5. Click **Import … posts**. Duplicate posts are not created twice.

Posts that are already published go to the history. Postaja writes the posts that have no text.

If you add a channel later, click **Import the rest**.

## Bulk creation

You can make texts and images for many posts at once: for a day (in the plan), for a brand (next 7 or 30 days, or all planned), or for a whole imported plan (**Create the whole plan**).

Before starting you see a **cost preview**: how many texts and images, with which model, the expected and maximum price, and how much of the cap is left. You choose **Texts**, **Images** or **Texts and images** and click **Start**.

Creation runs in the background, so you can close the page. You see the progress in the **Creation** tab, where you can also stop it.

## Ideas

On the dashboard, click **Suggest ideas** next to a channel. Claude suggests topics from the CGP and pillars, without repeating posts from the last 180 days, and assigns them free slots of the channel. Tick the ones you like and click **Add ticked to the plan**.
